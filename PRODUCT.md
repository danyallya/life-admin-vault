# Product brief

Problem: Keep essential records and due dates together with local-first reminders.

Audience: households and individuals

## First release

One user role, one core record (records) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

Store metadata before files; encryption and device recovery must be designed before real documents are accepted.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
